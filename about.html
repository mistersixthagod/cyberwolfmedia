About

Two categories, one reason people need this site

Cybersecurity and AI tools are both moving faster than most people have time to track. CyberWolf Media exists to close that gap — with reviews from people who actually use the tools, not just read the spec sheet.

What we cover

We write about the digital security tools that keep freelancers, remote workers, and small businesses safe — VPNs, password managers, antivirus software, identity protection — alongside the AI productivity tools that help the same people get more done: writing assistants, automation, voice and video AI. Most sites pick one lane. We cover both, because in practice the same person buying a password manager is usually also evaluating an AI writing tool the same month.

How we choose what to recommend

  • Every tool is used hands-on before it's recommended — we don't publish "best of" lists built from press releases.
  • Ratings weigh real trade-offs: price, ease of use, actual security or privacy practices, and support quality — not which program pays the highest commission.
  • Affiliate relationships are disclosed clearly wherever they appear, not buried in a footer. See our full disclosure for details.
  • Pricing and features get rechecked periodically, since software terms change — if something's out of date, let us know.
